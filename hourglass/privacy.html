Privacy Policy

Hourglass: Timer & Stopwatch · updated November 9, 2026

Hourglass: Timer & Stopwatch does not collect any data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app does not send anything you enter to the developer or to any third party.

What stays on your device

  • Your timers, saved times, presets, stopwatch laps and sound choice are stored only on your device.
  • Alarms are scheduled with Apple's AlarmKit on your device. Siri requests are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; Hourglass receives only the timer you ask for.

Deleting the app removes this data from your device.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
