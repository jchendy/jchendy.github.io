Hourglass: Timer & Stopwatch

Timers that ring at full volume, even on silent, plus a stopwatch with laps.

Get help

Found a problem or have a question? Open an issue and describe what happened.

The timer doesn't ring loudly.

Check that Alarms are allowed for Hourglass in Settings > Apps > Hourglass. Without it, iOS won't let the timer ring at full volume.

How do I use Siri?

Say "Start a timer in Hourglass", or name one of your saved times: "Start Pasta in Hourglass".

How do I change the sound?

Tap the gear button on the Timers screen and pick a sound.
