Privacy Policy

Daily Goals Tracker Journal · updated November 9, 2026

Daily Goals Tracker Journal does not collect any data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app does not send anything you enter to the developer or to any third party.

What stays on your device

  • Your goals, check-ins, feelings and gratitude notes are stored only on your device, where the home screen widget can read them.
  • Journal text is never saved anywhere. It is erased as soon as the journal closes, you switch apps or the phone locks.

Deleting the app removes this data from your device.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
