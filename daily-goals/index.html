Daily Goals Tracker

Daily goals, feelings check-ins, gratitude notes and a self-deleting journal.

Get help

Found a problem or have a question? Open an issue and describe what happened.

How do I add or change goals?

Tap Edit on the day screen to add, rename, reorder, hide or delete goals. Hidden goals keep their history.

Can I get my journal text back?

No. The journal is designed never to save what you write, so there is nothing to recover.

Is my data backed up?

Your data lives only on this device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Privacy

The app collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
